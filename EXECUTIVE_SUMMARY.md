## 🚧 CHALLENGES & RISKS

### Technical Challenges
1. **Performance:** Rendering large datasets (10k+ rows) smoothly
2. **Drag-drop:** Optimizing with 100+ cards in pipeline
3. **Mobile UX:** Balancing completeness with simplicity
4. **AI Latency:** Implementing optimistic UI for AI responses
5. **State Management:** Complex filters + inline editing + real-time updates

### Mitigation Strategies
- ✅ Virtual scrolling for large lists (react-window)
- ✅ Debouncing/throttling for search and scroll
- ✅ Progressive disclosure on mobile (hide less critical fields)
- ✅ Skeleton loading states everywhere
- ✅ Optimistic updates + rollback on error
